# 30 · Special-relativistic particle dynamics

**Priority:** P3 · **Size:** M · **Area:** Physics

## Why
Fast charged particles need relativistic momentum. It pairs naturally with the Lorentz force and Boris pusher.

## Scope
- State in terms of momentum (or γv); a speed-of-light parameter c.
- Relativistic Boris pusher (or Vay / Higuera–Cary) for charged particles.
- Energy as γmc² in diagnostics.

## Depends on
09.

## Done when
- Gyration in uniform B has frequency qB/(γm) and constant γ to round-off.
- A particle accelerated by uniform E follows hyperbolic motion and never exceeds c.
