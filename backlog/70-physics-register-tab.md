# 70 · The Physics tab: the register as a reference

**Priority:** P2 · **Size:** M · **Area:** Experiment workbench (redesign)

## Why
Kinematics, energy loss, trajectories, orbits, correlation and response are what one looks at when a number looks
wrong, not stages of the experiment. They deserve a tab, as a reference, after Report.

## Scope
- The present Kinematics, Energy loss and Trajectories tabs and the γ tab's orbit, correlation and response
  panels, kept whole, under one tab in the order of the physics register (`docs/physics-register`).
- Every ? explanation (57) and every consequence line (64) links to the panel that shows its physics, and each
  panel links to the register page and the validation that stands behind it.
- Nothing on this tab needs a run; it is computed from the setup as today.

## Depends on
64, 65.

## Done when
- The four tabs' panels are all present under Physics (their figure tests pass unchanged).
- A ? on the Plan or Analysis tab opens the Physics panel it names.
