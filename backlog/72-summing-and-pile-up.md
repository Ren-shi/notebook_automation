# 72 · Summing and pile-up

**Priority:** P3 · **Size:** M · **Area:** Experiment workbench (physics)

## Why
Two γ rays of one decay in the same crystal sum (a sum peak, lost peak counts); two events within the pulse
shaping time pile up. Both matter at the rates a close γ detector and a strong source give, and the source runs
(53) and the real-statistics runs (63) are where they show.

## Scope
- Summing within a crystal for cascades (71) and for a calibration source's cascades, from the crystals' total
  efficiencies; a correction factor per line reported with the efficiency points.
- Pile-up from the rate and a shaping time (`[run] shaping_time`), as a loss and as a shoulder above each peak.

## Depends on
63, 71.

## Done when
- A ⁶⁰Co source run at a few centimetres shows the 2.5 MeV sum peak with the area the efficiencies predict;
  the efficiency points are corrected and say so.
