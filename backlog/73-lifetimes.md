# 73 · Lifetimes: decay in flight, stopped and shifted components

**Priority:** P3 · **Size:** M · **Area:** Experiment workbench (physics)

## Why
A state with a lifetime comparable to the slowing-down time decays partly inside the target or a backing: the
Doppler shift and broadening change (the DSAM line shape), and the correction made for emission at the target's
exit is wrong by a known amount. Parked in the overview; the run model makes it a view of the data.

## Scope
- The level scheme's lifetimes (50) and the stopping (42) give the velocity at the moment of emission; the γ
  chain emits along the slowing-down path.
- The spectra show the stopped and the shifted components; the analysis (68) can fit the lifetime from the line
  shape as a later step.

## Depends on
50, 55, 63.

## Done when
- A state with a lifetime of a few picoseconds in a thick target gives a line shape whose stopped share equals
  the fraction of decays after stopping, within statistics; a short lifetime gives item 55's result.
