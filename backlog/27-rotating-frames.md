# 27 · Rotating reference frames

**Priority:** P3 · **Size:** S · **Area:** Physics

## Why
The restricted three-body problem, Lagrange points, Foucault pendulums and weather-style flows are posed in
rotating frames.

## Scope
- A frame force adding Coriolis, centrifugal and Euler terms for a given Ω(t).
- The Jacobi constant as an observable for a uniformly rotating frame.
- Helpers to transform trajectories between inertial and rotating frames.

## Done when
- In the circular restricted three-body problem, a particle at L4 with small mass ratio stays bounded and the
  Jacobi constant is conserved to the integrator's order.
- A Foucault pendulum precesses at Ω sin(latitude).
